The LULC raster, class statistics, charts and exports are spatially restricted to the selected Region, District or Ward. With no administrative filter, the analysis covers the full raster. Area calculations are reported only when the raster projection is a supported metre-based UTM coordinate system.
The repository contains six classified years: 2000, 2005, 2010, 2015, 2020 and 2025. Annual trends compare these available snapshots; they are not observations for every intervening year.
